# WC26 Promo — Production Script

## INTRO (0:00 – 0:15)
* **Pacing:** Ultra-fast cuts, aggressive sync to the beat, heavy screen shake on impact words.
* **Color Grade:** Cinematic high-contrast, slight vignette, glowing white captions.

### 0:00 – 0:04
* **Voiceover:** "The World Cup is here... and football history is about to be completely rewritten."
* **Visual Direction:** Start with a dark pitch-black background. Fast 3D zoom-in on the Official WC26 Logo. On the word "rewritten," trigger a glitch effect over a stadium packed with roaring fans.
* **On-Screen Text:** "THE WORLD CUP IS HERE" ➡️ "HISTORY REWRITTEN" (Bold, impact font, neon green/white glow).
* **SFX:** Sub-bass drop + deep cinematic riser + synthetic camera shutter glitch.

### 0:04 – 0:08
* **Voiceover:** "Only 8 nations have ever won the trophy, but this year, the ultimate curse is breaking."
* **Visual Direction:** Fast flashing sequence of the 8 historical champion flags (Brazil, Germany, Argentina, etc.) in grayscale. Cut abruptly to a high-contrast glowing shot of the World Cup Trophy shattering a literal glass overlay.
* **On-Screen Text:** "ONLY 8 NATIONS" ➡️ "CURSE IS BREAKING" (Red alert text shake).
* **SFX:** Glass shattering effect + metallic impact hit.

### 0:08 – 0:15
* **Voiceover:** "Five rising empires are ready to storm the stage and claim their first-ever golden crown. Here is who to watch."
* **Visual Direction:** Rapid-fire 0.5-second split-screen teases of the 5 featured teams (Ecuador, Japan, Morocco, Colombia, Netherlands) flashing their flags across the screen. End on a slow motion zoom-out transition to prime the next section.
* **On-Screen Text:** "5 RISING EMPIRES" ➡️ "FIRST GOLDEN CROWN" (Gold colored font warp).
* **SFX:** Whoosh transitions + collective stadium crowd chant swelling up.

## 1. ECUADOR (0:15 – 0:32)
* **Pacing:** Rigid, heavy, syncs visuals to structural rhythmic beats to mimic a "brick wall."
* **Color Grade:** Deep yellow and deep navy undertones.

### 0:15 – 0:21
* **Voiceover:** "First, Ecuador. Boasting an incredible 19-match unbeaten run, their defense is a solid brick wall..."
* **Visual Direction:** Drop a large Ecuador Crest/Flag as a background layout overlay. Slide in a bold "19 MATCHES UNBEATEN" counter ticking up rapidly. Layer a literal translucent brick texture pattern flashing for a frame over the timeline.
* **On-Screen Text:** "19-MATCH UNBEATEN" ➡️ "BRICK WALL" (Bold block tracking).
* **SFX:** Concrete heavy thud + ticking clock layer.

### 0:21 – 0:26
* **Voiceover:** "...led by Champions League winner Willian Pacho and Arsenal's Piero Hincapié."
* **Visual Direction:** Side-by-side portrait slide-in. Left side: Willian Pacho portrait with high-speed tracking lines. Right side: Piero Hincapié clean portrait masking out onto the timeline frame.
* **On-Screen Text:** "PACHO" & "HINCAPIE" (Clean name tags flashing on bottom-thirds).
* **SFX:** Sharp whip-whooshes for each player slide-in.

### 0:26 – 0:32
* **Voiceover:** "Shielded by Chelsea's Moisés Caicedo, they are incredibly tough to break down, though clinical finishing remains their biggest hurdle."
* **Visual Direction:** Center mask cut-out of Moisés Caicedo wearing a Chelsea kit. Zoom in towards his eyes on "tough to break down." Suddenly transition to a soccer ball hitting a goalpost on "biggest hurdle."
* **On-Screen Text:** "SHIELDED" ➡️ "TOUGH TO BREAK" ➡️ "BIGGEST HURDLE" (Red drop shadow).
* **SFX:** Heavy metallic clang (ball hitting post).

## 2. JAPAN (0:32 – 0:47)
* **Pacing:** Hyper-speed editing, frantic neon light motion trails, high-intensity frame rates.
* **Color Grade:** Neon samurai blue, stark clean white flashes.

### 0:32 – 0:37
* **Voiceover:** "Next, Japan. Despite losing injured superstars Kaoru Mitoma and Takumi Minamino, their high-intensity press remains deadly."
* **Visual Direction:** Display the Japan Flag filling the screen, immediately crossed out with a red glowing "X" mark over portraits of Mitoma and Minamino. Instantly shatter into multiple split screens tracking aggressive forward running motions.
* **On-Screen Text:** "JAPAN" ➡️ "INJURED SUPERSTARS" ➡️ "HIGH-INTENSITY PRESS" (Italicized kinetic speed font).
* **SFX:** Cybernetic slash sound + heartbeat sound ramping up fast.

### 0:37 – 0:42
* **Voiceover:** "...powered by Wataru Endo, creative genius Takefusa Kubo..."
* **Visual Direction:** Clean hard-cut transition. Left half of screen illuminates Wataru Endo looking focused. Right half dynamically scales up a vibrant, stylized portrait cutout of Takefusa Kubo. Add electric blue arcs jumping between both players.
* **On-Screen Text:** "ENDO: THE ANCHOR" ➡️ "KUBO: THE GENIUS" (Glow-in-the-dark blue tracking text).
* **SFX:** Electronic lightning buzzes.

### 0:42 – 0:47
* **Voiceover:** "...and top scorer Ayase Ueda, this highly disciplined squad is a tactical nightmare."
* **Visual Direction:** Slide down to focus on striker Ayase Ueda. Run a radar/scanning graphic circle sweep around his face on the words "tactical nightmare," while showing tactical whiteboard formations rapidly moving in the background layout.
* **On-Screen Text:** "UEDA" ➡️ "TACTICAL NIGHTMARE" (Glitch text overlay distortion).
* **SFX:** Digital sonar ping or warning alarm sound effect.

## 3. MOROCCO (0:47 – 1:00)
* **Pacing:** Epic, rhythmic, fluid transitions utilizing majestic desert sands or star trails tracking.
* **Color Grade:** Royal crimson red and rich deep emerald greens.

### 0:47 – 0:52
* **Voiceover:** "Third, Morocco. Under youth champion coach Mohamed Ouabi, the Atlas Lions have been transformed."
* **Visual Direction:** Drop the striking Morocco Flag behind a centered sharp-focused cut-out of head coach Mohamed Ouahbi. Throw an animated lion-roar silhouette masking out from the shadows directly behind him.
* **On-Screen Text:** "MOROCCO" ➡️ "ATLAS LIONS" ➡️ "TRANSFORMED" (Bold green text on crimson stroke).
* **SFX:** Deep cinematic low-frequency rumble + subtle distance lion roar sound.

### 0:52 – 1:00
* **Voiceover:** "...integrating fresh young talent alongside Achraf Hakimi and Yassine Bounou. They shift to a progressive wing attack to stun the world again."
* **Visual Direction:** Swipe left to show a clean cutout profile frame of Achraf Hakimi. Bounce the framing down onto goalkeeper Yassine Bounou (Bono) making a dramatic leaping block save background setup. Overlay dynamic neon lines moving fast up the wings of a digital field mesh overlay.
* **On-Screen Text:** "HAKIMI" ➡️ "BONO" ➡️ "STUN THE WORLD AGAIN" (Explosive font pop).
* **SFX:** Arrow whoosh whistling past + thunderous stadium explosion sound.

## 4. COLOMBIA (1:00 – 1:13)
* **Pacing:** Upbeat, danceable sync speed, fiery frame shakes, rhythmic velocity jumps.
* **Color Grade:** Bright saturated sun-gold yellows and high contrast blacks.

### 1:00 – 1:05
* **Voiceover:** "Fourth, Colombia. The ultimate dark horse enters the tournament on a legendary run under Néstor Lorenzo."
* **Visual Direction:** Fade in a dark stylized horse silhouette glowing neon yellow. Instantly flash into a picture of manager Néstor Lorenzo pointing directly at the camera lenses with a massive fire particle layer behind him.
* **On-Screen Text:** "COLOMBIA: DARK HORSE" ➡️ "LORENZO" (Chiseled metallic texture text).
* **SFX:** Horse whinny/stomp audio effect mixed into an industrial bass kick downbeat.

### 1:05 – 1:13
* **Voiceover:** "The creative genius of James Rodríguez and the electric pace of Luis Díaz bring elite momentum to North America."
* **Visual Direction:** Split vertical screen. Left side: James Rodríguez framed mid-celebration or action shot. Right side: Jump-cut the existing Luis Díaz asset with a custom lightning strike graphic flashing over his shoes to denote "electric pace."
* **On-Screen Text:** "JAMES: GENIUS" ➡️ "DIAZ: ELECTRIC PACE" (Yellow lightning text effects).
* **SFX:** High voltage electricity crackling + swift wind storm sounds.

## 5. NETHERLANDS (1:13 – 1:28)
* **Pacing:** Dramatic, heavy symphonic build, massive operatic stakes feel, imposing framing elements.
* **Color Grade:** Vibrant royal orange tint over stark industrial concrete-gray settings.

### 1:13 – 1:20
* **Voiceover:** "Finally, the Netherlands. Three-time runners-up, the Oranje are desperate to break their historic curse."
* **Visual Direction:** Display the Netherlands Flag. Flash silver metallic numbers "1974", "1978", and "2010" across the timeline screen before turning into a dark chained padlocked graphic that shatters on screen.
* **On-Screen Text:** "3-TIME RUNNERS-UP" ➡️ "BREAK THE CURSE" (Distressed heavy broken fonts).
* **SFX:** Heavy metal chains clanking together + orchestral drop.

### 1:20 – 1:28
* **Voiceover:** "Captained by the towering Virgil van Dijk, this highly pragmatic side is packed with quality, seeking their final shot at eternal glory."
* **Visual Direction:** Low-angle camera perspective scrolling upwards on an imposing cutout frame of Virgil van Dijk staring down the lens to accentuate "towering." Slowly pull the scene into a golden aura light filtering through from behind him on "eternal glory."
* **On-Screen Text:** "TOWERING VAN DIJK" ➡️ "ETERNAL GLORY" (Gleaming white gold shine gradient text).
* **SFX:** Deep church bell gong + heavenly ambient angelic choir synth drone.

## OUTRO (1:28 – 1:33)
* **Pacing:** Maximum loop retention optimization, quick call-to-actions, snappy animations.
* **Color Grade:** High saturation commercial pop coloring.

### 1:28 – 1:33
* **Voiceover:** "Only one question remains: Who will make history and lift their first World Cup trophy? Drop your champion in the comments and subscribe for more daily coverage."
* **Visual Direction:** Bring back the Official Transparent WC26 Logo in the center. Animate the 5 country flags spinning rapidly around the frame. Slide a massive glowing animated up-arrow element tracking onto the Comment Box / Subscribe graphic interface mockup.
* **On-Screen Text:** "WHO WINS?" ➡️ "COMMENT BELOW" ➡️ "SUBSCRIBE" (Pulsing neon green and red colors).
* **SFX:** Popping bubble alert chime + click button indicator overlay sound.
